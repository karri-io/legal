# Karri Legal and Compliance

This repository contains the official legal documents, policies, and terms of service for Karri.

## Why is this public?

We are building a secure, screenless voice communicator for children. We believe that in this space, data privacy is not just a regulatory checklist but the foundation of the trust parents place in our product.

To ensure complete transparency, we maintain our legal policies in this public repository. By using version control, we guarantee that the history of our privacy practices is fully auditable. Anyone can inspect the commit history to see exactly what has changed, when it changed, and how it impacts the handling of user data over time.

## Repository Structure

* `privacy-policy.md` - Details how we collect, store, and protect user data.
* `terms-and-conditions.md` - Details the basis on which Karri.io Ltd provides its services.

## Contributions and Contact

Because these documents represent binding legal agreements and regulatory compliance, this repository operates in a read-only capacity. We do not accept Pull Requests or GitHub Issues.

If you have questions, concerns, or feedback regarding our privacy practices, please contact us directly at [privacy@karri.io](mailto:privacy@karri.io).
